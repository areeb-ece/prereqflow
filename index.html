Import courses

One course per line: CODE Name (prerequisites). Commas separate required courses, or marks alternatives, Coreq: marks corequisites. A line such as 2-1 or Year 2 Semester 1 sets the semester for the courses after it; otherwise the course level is used (2000-level → 2-1). Paste many lines at once and each course lands on its own row. Enter adds a row.
